**Receipts across engines.** A receipt issued by the published 4.2.0 names
engine `@uwmd/verify` 0.2.0 and Protocol 4.1.0; 4.2.0 verifies it and reports
a tampered copy `failed` (`RCP-03`). The published 4.1.0 (`@uwmd/verify`
0.1.0) verifies the agreeing 4.2.0 receipt and reports the tampered copy
`unverifiable` (`RCP-07`, `RCP-03`); 4.2.0 does the same with a 4.1.0-issued
receipt. That is UW Receipt §5.3 as specified: a disagreement across two
engine identities is not attributed to the document.
